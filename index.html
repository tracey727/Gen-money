GENEVIEVE APP

Budget App

This app combines budgeting with waste review. Record money honestly, set category limits, protect upcoming bills, and use the Genevieve warning colours before overspending.

GREEN

Within plan. Spending remains comfortably inside the category limit.

YELLOW

Watch. The category is getting close to its limit or safe daily spending is low.

RED

Stop and review. Budget is nearly exhausted, exceeded, or protected money would be used.
