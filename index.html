How to use Every Cent

1. Add your accounts.

Tell the app what bank accounts, cash, savings, credit cards and debts you currently have.

2. Tell it what happened.

Use “I spent money”, “I received money” or “I moved money”. Transfers between your own accounts do not count as spending.

3. Judge spending simply.

Mark spending Essential, Worth it, Unsure or Waste. You can change your mind later.

4. Rescue unknown subscriptions.

Add anything you spot in your bank or email, even if you do not know what it is. Mark it Unknown and the app keeps it visible.

5. Back it up.

This first version saves to this browser/device. Use Download backup regularly so you can restore your information later.
